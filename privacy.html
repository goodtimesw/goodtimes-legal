資料由您掌握：好時光協助您整理健康紀錄、回顧變化，並準備與醫師討論的摘要。紀錄主要保存在裝置上；您可另外選擇 iCloud 備份、匯出檔案或伴侶分享。不同功能保存的範圍不同；伴侶摘要與您傳送的訊息會經由 Apple CloudKit 儲存及傳送。我們不販賣您的個人資料，也不將健康資料用於廣告追蹤。

一、我們蒐集什麼資料

好時光是一款經期追蹤與健康記錄 App。您輸入的資料（包括經期記錄、症狀、情緒、基礎體溫、體重、睡眠、LH / 排卵試紙、哺乳與產後記錄等）主要儲存於：

  • 您的裝置，包括支援的 iPhone、iPad 或 Mac
  • 您的個人 iCloud 私有資料庫（若您啟用 App 內的 iCloud 備份，範圍見第三節）
  • 好時光的 CloudKit 分享服務（若您使用伴侶分享或傳送訊息，範圍見第四節）

我們不會出售您的資料，也不會將您的健康資料用於廣告追蹤。

二、HealthKit 資料

在支援的裝置上，好時光可選擇性地與 Apple 健康（HealthKit）整合。經您明確授權後，可讀取以下類型的健康資料以帶入既有紀錄並進行週期與健康趨勢分析：

  • 月經流量紀錄
  • 子宮頸黏液
  • 排卵試紙（LH）結果
  • 性行為紀錄與保護措施欄位；匯入前請核對預覽及來源紀錄
  • 基礎體溫（BBT）與一般體溫
  • 體重
  • 相容 Apple Watch 已產生的睡眠手腕溫度資料

好時光不讀取 Apple 健康的「睡眠分析」資料，也不自行測量手腕溫度。您仍可在 App 手動記錄睡眠。

取得您允許的寫入權限後，手動「同步到 Apple 健康」及編輯紀錄後的自動補寫，可寫入月經流量、子宮頸黏液、支援的排卵試紙結果（陰性／LH 陽性）、基礎體溫、體重，並刪除、重寫本 App 先前建立的相關樣本。性行為、一般體溫與手腕溫度不會寫回；並非每種資料都支援雙向傳送。整批還原、匯入或清除 App 紀錄，不代表會清除 Apple 健康的樣本。

HealthKit 存取需經授權，用於匯入、記錄整理、估計與健康趨勢分析，不用於廣告或行銷。您可在系統的健康權限設定調整允許項目。若啟用伴侶分享，您選擇的摘要可能反映匯入紀錄所產生的估計；匯出 JSON、PDF 或試算表時，也可能包含匯入的值，請確認分享範圍。

自動匯入（選用功能）

分類資料自動匯入預設為關閉。當您在 Apple 健康自動匯入設定開啟某個類型時：

  • 分類資料在 App 開啟時檢查。這裡的經期、黏液與 LH 分類資料自動匯入，在 App 進入背景時停止檢查；不代表所有健康資料都沒有背景處理。
  • 沒有衝突的新紀錄自動補入。若當天該欄位是空的，Apple 健康的紀錄會自動匯入並標記來源。
  • 有衝突時不覆蓋。若當天已有您的紀錄且與 Apple 健康不同，新值會進入「待確認」清單，由您選擇：使用 Apple 健康 / 使用好時光 / 忽略。既有紀錄不會被自動覆蓋。

  • 僅讀取，不回寫。自動匯入只「讀」Apple 健康，不會把資料寫回。
  • 狀態留在裝置上。這項分類資料自動匯入的狀態（偏好設定、讀取進度、待確認佇列、抑制紀錄）保存在受完整檔案保護、排除備份的裝置檔案，不納入 App 內的 iCloud 備份、App Group 或 Widget。

數值與手腕溫度是不同的路徑：基礎體溫、一般體溫、體重及手腕溫度可在開啟 App 或手動同步時帶入。手腕溫度另有增量讀取；請勿依賴 3.6 在 App 關閉或鎖定時即時更新。實際更新受授權、資料可用性、裝置狀態與系統限制影響。本頁不把尚未發佈的背景更新修正描述為現有功能，也不代表整份健康紀錄會上傳至分享服務。

三、備份、匯出與還原

App 內的 iCloud 備份

這項選用功能使用您 Apple 帳號的 CloudKit 私有資料庫。先手動建立第一份備份，之後在符合條件的紀錄變更與開啟 App 時更新；它不是多台裝置逐筆即時合併的服務。

  • 會備份：可備份的每日紀錄、產後健康狀態、HRT 療程、伴侶聊天隱藏時間等健康與相關設定資料。「我的句子」隨符合條件的備份一起保存；修改句子會要求更新，但仍需已有雲端基準、iCloud 可用，且有可備份的每日紀錄或用藥事件。只有句子不能單獨建立原本不符合條件的備份。
  • 不會備份：從 Apple 健康匯入的值、LH 試紙照片、使用者名稱、提醒時間、通知設定、伴侶聊天訊息快取、外觀／語言等 App 偏好與分享設定。某天 LH 含匯入來源時，該天所有 LH 紀錄都排除；體重只有確認為手動輸入才納入；去掉匯入值後為空白的每日紀錄也不納入。
  • 還原：用雲端內容整份取代這台裝置的每日紀錄與用藥事件，不會逐筆合併；這台裝置上從 Apple 健康匯入的值也會移除，需再匯入。不會刪除 Apple 健康資料庫中的原始樣本。HRT、產後狀態與聊天隱藏時間依備份取代；「我的句子」只在備份有此欄位時取代。使用者名稱、提醒、通知設定與聊天快取保留。

Apple 提供雲端傳輸與儲存保護；好時光沒有另加應用層的端對端加密。關閉 App 的 iCloud 備份不等於刪除既有雲端內容，也不等於關閉 iOS／iPadOS 等系統備份。系統備份是另外一項由您與 Apple 系統設定管理的功能，可能包含符合備份條件的本機資料。

舊版本建立的備份或分享可能保留當時的欄位；更新 App 不表示所有舊雲端副本已被清理。若您希望移除既有內容，請依第九節分別操作並確認結果。

備份檔（JSON）、PDF 與試算表

JSON 可保存每日紀錄（含從 Apple 健康匯入的值）、用藥事件、使用者名稱、產後／HRT 與通知設定、伴侶對話本機快取等。照片僅包含匯出時讀得到、且被紀錄引用的 LH 試紙照片。不包含「我的句子」、外觀／語言偏好、健康授權、Face ID、購買／試用狀態與 iCloud 備份設定；不是所有資料的完整副本。

對話快取中的分享碼會在 JSON 裡，但匯入不會重建伴侶分享設定。匯入會整份取代目前的每日紀錄與用藥事件，不合併；檔案含聊天快取時也取代快取。其他支援的設定在檔案有值時才套用，使用者名稱非空才取代；未包含的「我的句子」不變。

JSON 是未加密的明文檔案。PDF 與試算表也可能含健康紀錄。請只保存到信任的位置，傳送前先檢查內容；您選擇的檔案或郵件服務會依其政策處理收到的資料。刪除 App 無法收回已匯出的檔案或對方保存的副本。

  • 停止與清理：您可停止分享或更換分享碼。App 會嘗試清理相關雲端資料；分享者可刪除自己建立的摘要與訊息，但對方建立的訊息或查看者紀錄可能只有建立者或管理端能刪除。權限限制不會只靠重試消失；請依清理提示確認尚存內容，必要時聯絡支援。按下停止不代表雲端已全部刪除。

隱私權政策

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This page describes the currently released version 3.6. Features and stored content may differ on other platforms or older versions. Unreleased features are not represented as available.

Your data, under your control: GoodTimes helps you organise health records, review changes and prepare summaries for discussions with your clinician. Records are primarily stored on your device. iCloud backup, file exports and partner sharing are optional and preserve different information. Partner summaries and messages you send are stored and transmitted through Apple CloudKit. We do not sell your personal data or use health data for advertising tracking.

1. What Data We Collect

GoodTimes is a cycle tracking and health logging app. The data you enter, including period records, symptoms, mood, basal body temperature, weight, sleep, LH / ovulation test records, breastfeeding, and postpartum records, is primarily stored in:

  • Your device, including supported iPhone, iPad or Mac models
  • Your personal iCloud Private Database, if you enable in-app iCloud backup (section 3)
  • GoodTimes' CloudKit sharing service, if you use partner sharing or messaging (section 4)

We do not sell your data or use your health data for advertising tracking.

2. HealthKit Data

On supported devices, GoodTimes may optionally integrate with Apple Health (HealthKit). With your explicit permission, it can read the following types of health data to bring in your existing records and support cycle and health trend analysis:

  • Menstrual flow records
  • Cervical mucus
  • Ovulation test (LH) results
  • Sexual activity records and protection fields; check the preview against the source before importing
  • Basal body temperature (BBT) and body temperature
  • Weight
  • Sleeping wrist temperature data already generated by a compatible Apple Watch

GoodTimes does not read Apple Health's sleep-analysis data or measure wrist temperature itself. You can still record sleep manually in the app.

With the write permissions you allow, manual Sync to Apple Health and automatic rewrites after record edits may write menstrual flow, cervical mucus, supported ovulation test results (negative / LH positive), basal body temperature and weight, including deleting and rewriting related samples previously created by this app. Sexual activity, general body temperature and wrist temperature are not written back. Not every type supports two-way transfer. Replacing, restoring or clearing app records does not mean Apple Health samples are cleared.

HealthKit access requires permission and supports import, record organisation, estimates and health trend analysis, not advertising or marketing. Manage allowed types in the system's health permissions. If you enable partner sharing, selected summaries may reflect estimates derived from imported records. JSON, PDF and spreadsheet exports may also include imported values; check what you choose to share.

Auto-import (optional)

Category auto-import is OFF by default. When you enable a type in Apple Health auto-import settings:

  • Category checks while the app is open. This period, mucus and LH category auto-import stops checking in the background. That does not mean all health-data processing is foreground-only.
  • Conflict-free records are added automatically. If a field for a given day is empty, Apple Health's value is imported and tagged with its source.
  • Conflicts never overwrite. If a day already has your record and it differs from Apple Health, the new value goes into a pending review list where you choose: Use Apple Health / Use GoodTimes / Ignore. Existing records are never overwritten automatically.
  • Sexual activity is always reviewed through the preview flow, never auto-imported.
  • Read-only. Auto-import only reads from Apple Health; it never writes back.
  • State stays on the device. This category auto-import's preferences, read progress, review queue and suppressions are stored in protected, backup-excluded device files, not in in-app iCloud backup, App Group data or widgets.

Numeric and wrist-temperature imports are separate. Basal body temperature, general body temperature, weight and wrist temperature can be imported when you open the app or sync manually. Wrist temperature also uses incremental reads. Do not rely on version 3.6 to update immediately while the app is closed or the device is locked. Updates depend on permission, available data, device state and system limitations. This page does not present unreleased background-update fixes as existing features, or imply that your entire health log is uploaded to the sharing service.

3. Backup, Export and Restore

In-app iCloud backup

This optional feature uses your Apple account's CloudKit Private Database. Create the first backup manually; eligible record changes and app openings can then update it. It is not a live, record-by-record merge across devices.

  • Included: Eligible daily records, postpartum health state, HRT episodes, partner-chat hidden timestamps and related supported health/settings data. My Phrases accompanies eligible uploads. Editing phrases requests an update, but uploading still requires an established cloud baseline, available iCloud and eligible daily records or medication events. Phrases alone do not make an otherwise ineligible device able to upload.
  • Excluded: Values imported from Apple Health, LH test photos, user name, reminder times, notification settings, cached partner-chat messages, appearance/language preferences and sharing settings. If a day's LH contains an imported source, all LH records for that day are excluded. Only weight known to be entered manually is included. Days left empty after removing imported values are omitted.
  • Restoring: Replaces this device's daily records and medication events as a whole, without merging, including removal of imported Apple Health values on this device. Import those values again afterward. It does not delete original samples in the Apple Health database. HRT, postpartum state and chat hidden timestamps are replaced from the backup. My Phrases is replaced only when that field exists. User name, reminders, notification settings and chat cache are retained.

Apple provides cloud transmission and storage protection; GoodTimes adds no app-level end-to-end encryption. Turning off in-app iCloud backup does not delete existing cloud content or turn off iOS/iPadOS and other system backups. System backup is separate, managed through your Apple system settings, and may include eligible local app data.

Backups or shares created by older versions may retain the fields saved at that time. Updating the app does not mean all older cloud copies have been cleaned up. To remove existing content, follow the separate steps in section 9 and check their results.

JSON backups, PDFs and spreadsheets

JSON can preserve daily records (including imported Apple Health values), medication events, user name, postpartum/HRT and notification settings, and the local partner-conversation cache. LH photos are included only if referenced by a record and readable at export. My Phrases, appearance/language preferences, health permissions, Face ID, purchase/trial status and iCloud backup settings are excluded. It is not a complete copy of everything.

Conversation-cache sharing codes remain in the JSON, but importing does not restore partner-sharing settings. Import replaces current daily records and medication events rather than merging them; it also replaces chat cache when that field is present. Other supported settings are applied only when present, and a nonempty user name replaces the current name. Excluded My Phrases remains unchanged.

JSON files are unencrypted plain text. PDFs and spreadsheets may also contain health records. Save them only somewhere you trust and review their contents before sending them. File or email services you choose handle received data under their own policies. Deleting the app cannot recall exported files or copies saved by someone else.

4. Partner Sharing

You can generate a sharing code, select summaries and send messages to another user. This is separate from your personal iCloud backup:

  • Opt-in sharing: You must actively generate and share a code.
